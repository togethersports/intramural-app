01 — Foundation

Intramural makes school leagues feel official.

Positioning

The league software students run themselves. Drafts, schedules that fit lunch periods, live stats, playoffs — the whole season, no clipboard.

Audience

Student commissioners and captains first; athletic directors and advisors second. Built for a phone in a hallway, trusted in a front office.

Promise

Zero to drafted-and-scheduled in under 20 minutes. Free for schools. Every stat, trade, and tiebreaker on the record.

Earnest
The season matters. We never wink at it.
Specific
"Lunch A · Gym 1", not "flexible scheduling".
On the record
Receipts, logs, tiebreakers. Nothing hand-waved.
Competitive
Stats worth arguing about. We take sides on rigor.
02 — The mark

The Bracket

Two seeds feed a matchup; one line comes out the other side in Whistle Red. It reads as a bracket, a schedule, and a decision at once — and it is the only place red appears in the identity by default.

Grid
Drawn on a 64 × 64 unit square. Inputs at y18 / y46, output at y32.
Stroke
6 units, round caps. Fixed ratio — never re-weight by eye.
Clearspace
28 units (the connector height) on all four sides.
Minimum
20 px on screen, 7 mm in print. Below that, drop the red line.

03 — Lockups & misuse

Lockups, sizes, and what not to do

Intramural
Intramural
Lockup: text 0.53× mark width, gap 0.28× · mark 48 / 32 / 20 px
Never
  • Re-weight, stretch, or rotate the strokes
  • Mirror the mark so the output points left
  • Add a third input or a second output line
  • Colour the input lines red — red is the output only
  • Set the black mark on Whistle Red
  • Add gradients, shadows, or outer glows
  • Re-space or re-typeset the wordmark
  • Merge the mark into a school crest or badge
App icon

Full-bleed square, Night Court ground, white strokes, red output. No rounding in the file — iOS masks it.

One colour

For single-ink print or embroidery use mark-red or all-white. The output line simply matches.

Co-branding

"Intramural at [School]" — marks side by side, 1px divider, equal optical height. Never combined.

Ratio

60% Court Blue · 30% cream and white · 8% Night Court · 2% Whistle Red. Two background colours per surface, maximum.

Contrast

Text on Court Blue is white at 500 or heavier. Never Night Court body copy on Court Blue. Red is never a text colour on cream below 17px.

School colours

Team colours live inside team badges and bracket rows only — never applied to Intramural chrome or buttons.

07 — Voice

Sound like the best commissioner in the school

We write
"Zero to drafted-and-scheduled in under 20 minutes."
"Two taps per event, undo anything."
"Warriors send Cohen to Hawks for Levy and Katz."
"Built for lunch periods everywhere."
We don't
"Revolutionize your athletic engagement ecosystem."
"Let's get this bread"
"Seamless, powerful, best-in-class scheduling."
"Oops! Something went wrong."
Verbs first
Start a league. Propose a trade. Lock the deadline.
Real nouns
Gyms, periods, seeds, picks — not "resources".
No emoji
Ever. The stat chips carry the energy instead.
Errors name the fix
"Gym 1 is taken at Lunch A. Pick another slot."
